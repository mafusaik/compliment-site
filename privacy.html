Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Compliment is engineered from the ground up to respect your personal boundaries.

Effective Date: November 2026 · Developer: GlazerDev · Applicable to: Compliment Mobile App

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Compliment mobile application (the "App"), developed and published by GlazerDev ("we", "us", or "our").

We believe great mobile experiences should never come at the expense of your privacy. Compliment is designed to work entirely locally on your smartphone. It does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.

2. Information Collection and Use

Data Collection Summary (Zero Data Sent to Servers)

Compliment does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

All app settings—such as your chosen notification time and notification toggles—are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences on Android / UserDefaults on iOS). We do not operate remote databases, analytics dashboards, or user tracking infrastructure for this application.

A. Information We Do Not Collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • No Device or Hardware IDs: We do not collect IMEI, MAC address, Advertising Identifier (IDFA/AAID), or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: We do not access or record your GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular network location.
  • No Behavioral or Analytics Tracking: We do not integrate third-party tracking SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Facebook Pixel, or AppsFlyer).

B. Optional Direct Inquiries:

If you choose to contact us directly via email (e.g., at glazer.dev@gmail.com) for support, bug reports, or compliment suggestions, we will receive your email address and any message content you provide. This information is used strictly to answer your question and is never added to marketing lists or shared with third parties.

3. Device Permissions Required

To provide its core functionality, Compliment requests only the minimal necessary device permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / UNUserNotificationCenter): Required to show daily compliment notifications on your device screen at your selected time.
  • Exact Alarm & Reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android): Allows the operating system to wake the local notification schedule precisely at the exact minute you specified, rather than being delayed by system battery queues.

You can grant, modify, or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

4. Third-Party Services and Distribution Platforms

The App is distributed via official digital storefronts (the Google Play Store and Apple App Store). These distribution platforms may collect basic, aggregated crash logs or install statistics in accordance with their own respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no data is stored on remote servers, you maintain complete ownership and control over your data at all times. Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage immediately and permanently removes all locally saved settings and schedules from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Compliment contains wholesome, positive affirmations suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), in full compliance with COPPA and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date". If we ever introduce optional cloud features, we will update this document, clearly outline what data is processed, and provide in-app disclosure.
