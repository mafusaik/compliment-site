Because Compliment works 100% on-device without remote servers or cloud accounts, notifications rely directly on your operating system's internal alarm scheduler. Modern devices have aggressive battery-saving modes that may delay or silence local alarms:

  • Check permissions: Confirm that notifications are allowed in your device settings for Compliment.
  • Check scheduled time: Open Compliment and ensure the daily time toggle is active and set to your desired hour.
  • Do Not Disturb / Focus: Make sure your device is not currently silencing incoming banners during scheduled Focus hours.

Follow the steps below depending on your operating system and manufacturer:

On Android (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS/MIUI, Pixel, OnePlus):

  1. Long press the Compliment app icon and tap App info (ⓘ).
  2. Tap Battery and select Unrestricted (or disable "Put app to sleep").
  3. On Xiaomi/Redmi devices: Enable Autostart and set Battery Saver to No restrictions.
  4. Tap Permissions and ensure Notifications and Alarms & Reminders are allowed.

On iOS (iPhone / iPad):

  1. Open Settings → Compliment → Notifications.
  2. Enable Allow Notifications and check Immediate Delivery (rather than "Scheduled Summary").
  3. If you use Focus modes (Sleep, Work), add Compliment to allowed apps under Settings → Focus.

You can change your scheduled delivery time at any moment:

  1. Open the Compliment app on your device.
  2. Tap the time selector on the main screen.
  3. Pick your new preferred hour and minute (e.g. 08:30 AM for breakfast, or 09:00 PM before bedtime).
  4. Confirm the change. The new schedule takes effect immediately for the next scheduled occurrence.

If you change phones or need to reinstall:

  • Simply download Compliment again from Google Play or the App Store.
  • When opening for the first time, grant the notification permission prompt.
  • Select your preferred notification time. Because no user login or password is required, the app is ready in less than 10 seconds.
